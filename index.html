Merle S. Robboy, MD

A board-certified obstetrician and gynecologist who practised in Orange County from 1972, taught at UC Irvine for three decades and chaired obstetrics and gynecology at Hoag. Now retired from medicine, still active as an orchid educator and dahlia judge, and working with an animal hospital.

About

Medicine and surgery, together

I chose obstetrics and gynecology because it brings medical care and surgical skill together in one specialty, and I found great fulfillment in delivering babies.

From 1972 I ran my own practice, Merle S. Robboy, MD, A Professional Corporation, and spent 46 years on the active staff of Hoag Memorial Hospital Presbyterian. Alongside patient care I taught obstetrics and gynecology at the University of California, Irvine, from 1972 to 2003.

I am retired from medicine now. These days I work with an animal hospital; I have always had pets, I love cats, and I enjoy working with people who care about animals.

Career

A career in women's health

My training took me from Ohio State to the U.S. Public Health Service, UCLA and City of Hope, and then to practice and teaching in Orange County.

Education and training

  • BA, Case Western Reserve University, 1961
  • MD, The Ohio State University College of Medicine, 1965
  • Rotating intern, U.S. Public Health Service Hospital, 1965 to 1966
  • Resident, UCLA Center for Health Sciences, 1968 to 1972
  • Resident in general and gynecologic surgery (oncology), City of Hope, 1972
  • Board certified, American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology
  • Fellow, American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists

Service to the profession

Leadership, societies and writing

Orange County Obstetrical and Gynecological Society

Treasurer 1980 to 1981, secretary 1981 to 1982, vice president 1982 to 1983 and president 1983 to 1984.

Professional societies

Pacific Coast Obstetrical and Gynecological Society, American Fertility Society, and AAGL (American Association of Gynecologic Laparoscopists).

Publications

Articles in The American Journal of Clinical Nutrition and in Obstetrics and Gynecology.

In 1976 I volunteered gynecological services at the SOS Clinic.

Beyond medicine

Orchids and dahlias

I lecture on orchids and contribute to local orchid groups as an educator. I am a lifetime member of the Newport Harbor Orchid Society and a member of the American Orchid Society, and I received the Diamond Award from the Orchid Digest.

I am also an accredited judge for the American Dahlia Society.
